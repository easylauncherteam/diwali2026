# 🪔 3D Ramayana Story Mode – Cinematic Diwali Experience & 3D Pattakas Arena

A premium, interactive, web-based 3D animated Ramayana storytelling and Diwali celebration experience built with **React**, **Three.js / React Three Fiber**, **Framer Motion**, and the **Web Audio API**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🎬 1. 3D Ramayana Story Mode (Sequential Animated Chapters)
Experience the epic Ramayana journey presented sequentially, scene by scene, like a high-budget 3D animated movie:

1. **SCENE 1: दशरथ और राम का वनवास (The Exile Begins)**
   - King Dasharath emotionally bidding farewell to Lord Ram, Mata Sita, and Lakshman.
   - Queen Kaushalya weeping, citizens of Ayodhya immersed in sorrow.
   - 3D Royal Palace architecture with golden pillars, royal throne, and torches.
   - Hindi Voiceover Narration: *"जब प्रभु श्री राम को चौदह वर्षों का वनवास मिला, तब पूरी अयोध्या शोक में डूब गई।"*

2. **SCENE 2: भरत-राम मिलन – चित्रकूट (Chitrakoot Reunion)**
   - Bharat arriving at Chitrakoot with Ayodhya's royal family.
   - Emotional reunion between the brothers.
   - Bharat requesting Ram to return to Ayodhya and receiving Ram's sacred Charan Padukas.
   - Lush 3D forest, Mandakini river, ancient trees, and natural god-ray lighting.
   - Hindi Voiceover Narration: *"भरत अपने बड़े भाई श्री राम को वापस अयोध्या ले जाने चित्रकूट पहुंचे, लेकिन राम ने अपने पिता के वचन का पालन किया।"*

3. **SCENE 3: सीता हरण (The Abduction & Jatayu's Valour)**
   - Swarna Mriga (Golden Deer) prancing in the forest.
   - Lord Ram pursuing the deer with his divine bow Kodanda.
   - Glowing golden Lakshman Rekha protecting the cottage.
   - Sage Ravana arriving in disguise, abducting Mata Sita in the Pushpak Viman.
   - Jatayu's heroic aerial combat in dark stormy skies with flashing lightning.
   - Hindi Voiceover Narration: *"वन में रावण ने छल से माता सीता का हरण कर लिया। जटायु ने उन्हें बचाने के लिए वीरतापूर्वक संघर्ष किया।"*

4. **SCENE 4: हनुमान-राम मिलन (The Sacred Bond)**
   - Lord Ram and Lakshman meeting Pawanputra Hanuman in Kishkindha.
   - Hanuman Ji bowing respectfully at Lord Ram's lotus feet in deep devotion.
   - Sugriva and Vanar Sena gathering on cliffs with saffron flags.
   - Divine golden aura, connecting celestial light beam, and falling flower petals (Pushpa Vrishti).
   - Hindi Voiceover Narration: *"तभी प्रभु श्री राम की भेंट उनके परम भक्त हनुमान जी से हुई। हनुमान जी ने माता सीता को खोजने का प्रण लिया।"*

5. **SCENE 5: रावण दहन (Victory of Dharma)**
   - Epic battle between Ram Ji's Sena and Ravana's demon army.
   - Lord Ram aiming the Brahmastra arrow from his golden battle chariot.
   - Colossal 10-headed Ravana effigy bursting into towering 3D procedural flames.
   - Rising embers, volcanic smoke, and night sky victory fireworks.
   - Hindi Voiceover Narration: *"अंततः धर्म की अधर्म पर विजय हुई। प्रभु श्री राम ने रावण का वध करके सत्य और धर्म की स्थापना की।"*

6. **SCENE 6: अयोध्या आगमन – Grand Diwali Finale**
   - Lord Ram, Mata Sita, Lakshman, and Hanuman Ji returning victorious to Ayodhya.
   - Grand 3D Ram Mandir with golden shikharas, flags, and royal balcony.
   - Millions of glowing Diyas illuminating terraces and ghats.
   - Saryu river water reflections and non-stop colourful fireworks.
   - Hindi Voiceover Narration: *"चौदह वर्षों के वनवास के बाद प्रभु श्री राम अयोध्या लौटे। उनके स्वागत में पूरी अयोध्या दीपों से जगमगा उठी। इसी पावन उत्सव को हम दीपावली के रूप में मनाते हैं।"*

### 💥 2. Extra Feature: Extreme Realistic 3D Pattakas Burning Experience
A realistic 3D ground & sky firecrackers simulation:
- **3D Ground Firecrackers**:
  - **शाही अनार (Anaar Fountain)**: High-altitude cone of golden/silver sparks with realistic gravity fall-off, glowing nozzle, and white smoke.
  - **जमीन चकरी (Chakri)**: High-RPM spinning ground wheel with spiral spark trails and fire ring.
  - **हजार लड़ी (Ladi)**: Sequential rapid staccato explosions with flying paper sparks and smoke puffs.
  - **गगन रॉकेट (Sky Rocket)**: Launch bottle stand, fiery exhaust trail, upward acceleration, and aerial peony burst.
  - **सुतली बम (Sutli Bomb)**: Burning fuse with green jute bomb, massive sub-bass boom, shockwave light, and thick dust cloud.
- **Interactive Features**:
  - Click any 3D cracker on the ground to ignite its fuse!
  - Matrix-style **Slow-Motion 0.25x** toggle to inspect explosion physics.
  - **Auto Loop Celebration** for continuous festive bursts.
  - Volumetric grey/white smoke with wind dispersal and lighting illumination.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **React 18** – Interactive UI & state orchestration
- **Three.js & @react-three/fiber** – 3D procedural geometries, PBR lighting, shadows, and particle simulations
- **@react-three/drei & @react-three/postprocessing** – Camera controls, bloom glow, and cinematic vignettes
- **Web Audio API** – 100% offline procedural synthesizer for Shankh, temple bells, dhol, sitar ragas, fireworks, and explosions
- **Web Speech Synthesis API** – Natural Hindi voice narration (`hi-IN`)
- **Framer Motion** – Smooth UI animations and transitions

## 🚀 Running Locally

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/easylauncherteam/diwali2026.git
   cd diwali2026
   ```

2. **Open in browser**

   Simply open `index.html` in your browser, or use a local dev server:

   ```bash
   # Using Python
   python3 -m http.server 8000

   # Using Node.js (npx)
   npx serve .
   ```

3. **Visit** `http://localhost:8000` in your browser
