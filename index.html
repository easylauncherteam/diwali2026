The Five Days of Diwali

Explore the timeless spiritual meaning, auspicious 2026 calendar dates, and sacred rituals of each day in the grand celebration.

Day 1 of 5

Dhanteras

Friday, November 6, 2026

Welcomes Lord Dhanvantari, the divine physician of health, and Goddess Lakshmi's sacred blessings of prosperity. Families illuminate thresholds with Yamadeep lamps and welcome new brass, gold, or silver utensils into pristine homes.

Rituals: Buying gold & brass utensils, lighting first evening Yamadeep.
Day 2 of 5

Naraka Chaturdashi

Saturday, November 7, 2026

Commemorates Lord Krishna and Satyabhama vanquishing darkness and liberating countless souls. Devotees take the auspicious pre-dawn herbal oil bath (Abhyanga Snan) and banish negativity with sparkling lamps and vibrant kolams.

Rituals: Pre-dawn ubtan herbal bath, lighting 14 diyas, joyful sparklers.
Main Day
Day 3 of 5 • Auspicious Peak

Lakshmi Puja

Sunday, November 8, 2026

The grand celestial night of lights when Goddess Lakshmi and Lord Ganesha are welcomed into sunlit, joyous homes. Windows and courtyards shine with countless clay diyas to guide wealth, wisdom, and peace across every threshold.

Rituals: Grand evening Lakshmi-Ganesha puja, family feast, rows of diyas.
Day 4 of 5

Govardhan Puja

Monday, November 9, 2026

Honors Lord Krishna lifting the Govardhan mountain to shelter nature and all living beings from storms. Devotees prepare 'Annakut'—mountains of sacred vegetarian dishes—in profound gratitude to mother earth and the harvest.

Rituals: 56-bhog Annakut offerings, cow worship, honoring nature's bounty.
Day 5 of 5

Bhai Dooj

Tuesday, November 10, 2026

Celebrates the lifelong bond of love, care, and protection between sisters and brothers, recalling Goddess Yamuna welcoming brother Yama with sweet feasts. Sisters apply sacred vermilion tilak with prayers for long, flourishing lives.

Rituals: Sister's auspicious tilak, exchanging handmade mithai & loving gifts.
